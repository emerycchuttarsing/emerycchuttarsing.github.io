## Engineering question

What are the components required to build and evaluate a small generative language model?

## Approach

The project connects TinyStories data preparation, BPE tokenization, a Transformer model, training and autoregressive generation.

## Evidence to present

The detailed study will show the model structure, recorded learning curves and generation examples, together with the limitations of the training setup.
